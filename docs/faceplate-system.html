Faceplate System

The Columns direction applied everywhere the user shapes sound: the three instruments, the six effect devices, a track's device chain and the mixer. The drum machine uses the pad section from Hero. Every control is a parameter the product already has. Press Play for a two-bar loop through all of it. The faders, wells, chains and mixer all act on what you hear.

Instruments

Each section is a column: the well on top, its faders beneath, one group rule over both. Drag directly on the wells, or click any value and type. The drum table opens the selected pad in place.

Chain

A track's sound runs top to bottom: the instrument, then each device in order, then the mixer. Every device card is the module's full 1000px, so a chain grows down the page like the instrument above it. Drag a card by its grip to reorder, or focus the grip and use the up and down arrow keys. Bypass, duplicate, reset and remove sit in each card's header, and Add device ends the stack.

More devices

The other three device types, as they sit on other tracks. Every card has the same layout: a header with the chain actions, then a well on the left four columns that draws what the device does, with its fader groups across the other eight. Each group's width follows its number of faders.

Mixer

The mixer spans the full width of the screen. Track strips are a fixed 104px, so more tracks fill the width before it scrolls, and the master is pinned to the right edge. Every strip keeps the same rows, so the controls line up across the whole desk: name, mute and solo, pan, fader with meter, and the chain it carries. The meters are live while the loop plays.

Parts

The whole system is built from these shared components. Nothing above is drawn any other way.

  • Module1000px panel on the 12-column grid, 20px padding, no borders. The mixer is the exception and spans the screen.
  • HeaderTrack bar and name, readouts as label over value, meter, one white primary action.
  • WellBlack frame for curves, waveforms and graphs. Title, live value, gridlines, a time or frequency scale.
  • Well controlA dot or handles drawn on the well and dragged directly. The faders stay the keyboard route.
  • Hairline fader1px track, 2px fill, 20 × 3px cap, Juno scale ticks, value above and label below. Laid on its side for dense rows: the pad table and pan.
  • Group ruleUppercase title followed by a line that spans the controls it groups.
  • SwitchA stack of options the height of a fader. The chosen one is a white fill.
  • Value fieldEvery readout next to a fader is a text field. Type 2.4k, 120ms, −6, L30, 4:1 or 1/8. and press Enter. Up and Down nudge the value, Escape cancels.
  • PickerFilled button with a caret that opens a menu, ending with Browse the library.
  • Device cardFull-width module: header with grip and actions, a well in four columns, fader groups in eight.
  • StripFixed rows so every strip lines up across the desk, fader and meter at the centre.
  • ColourMonochrome chrome. The track's colour is the ink for wells and meters, and nothing else.
